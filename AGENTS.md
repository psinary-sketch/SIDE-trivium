## What this repository is

The Lean 4 kernel formalizing the **Trivium Bijection**: the seven mechanism classes governing zeros of ξ(s) correspond to the seven nontrivial elements of (ℤ/2)³ generated by {−1, 2, 3} via squarefree quadratic discriminant structure. This is a structural identity, not coincidence — the same generators (−1, 2, 3) produce both the mechanism catalogue and the Fano plane PG(2, 𝔽₂).

The bijection compiles with **0 axioms whatsoever** — not even Lean's foundational three (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The proof reduces to decidability checks on seven-element finite types.

Member of the **PLACE TO STAND federation of kernels**. Independent: own toolchain pin, own Zenodo deposit. Cross-kernel content travels by vendoring-with-attribution, not via Lake dependencies.

## Theorems exported

Key exports in `Trivium/Bijection.lean`:

- **`MechanismClass`** — finite type with seven inhabitants, one per voice (C₁–C₇)
- **`QuadraticDiscriminant`** — finite type with seven inhabitants {−1, 2, −2, 3, −3, 6, −6}
- **`triviumBijection : MechanismClass ≃ QuadraticDiscriminant`** — the named bijection (an `Equiv` bundle of `mechanismToDiscriminant`, `discriminantToMechanism`, and the two inverse-pairing proofs)
- **`mechanism_class_card`** / **`quadratic_discriminant_card`** — both finite types have cardinality 7
- **`exponent_triple_nonzero`** / **`exponent_triple_injective`** — every discriminant maps to a non-zero element of (ℤ/2)³ via the squarefree-part exponent encoding, and the encoding is injective
- **`discriminant_values_distinct`** — the seven integer values are pairwise distinct
- **`all_discriminants_squarefree`** — each of the seven discriminants is squarefree
- **`trivium_theorem`** — the structural theorem: bijection exists, both cardinalities are 7

Component pairings established by the bijection:

- C₁ Schwarz ↔ d = −1 ↔ ℚ(i)
- C₂ Euler ↔ d = 2 ↔ ℚ(√2)
- C₃ Functional Equation ↔ d = −2
- C₄ Modular ↔ d = 3
- C₅ Spectral ↔ d = −3 (Eisenstein)
- C₆ Cauchy-Riemann ↔ d = 6
- C₇ Hadamard ↔ d = −6

The proofs reduce to `native_decide` or `cases ... <;> rfl` over the seven-element finite types.

## Discipline (for agents that propose edits)

1. **Federation respect.** No cross-kernel Lake dependencies.

2. **0 axioms whatsoever.** This kernel's distinguishing property is that even Lean's foundational three axioms are not used. Any PR that introduces axioms (including Lean core) breaks this property — flag clearly and justify.

3. **Patent gates.** Phase 2 papers building on this kernel — *Arithmetic Origin of Quantum Codes*, *Arithmetic Origin of Steane Code*, *Structural Error Correction* — are P-ZONE-gated or P-CODE-gated for publication. Do not draft publication-ready content from those papers without confirming patent status with the author.

4. **No "conditional" framing.** The bijection compiles unconditionally.

5. **I+D+S ordering.** Independence, Determination, Symmetry. SIDE: Symmetry, Independence, Determination, Exhaustiveness.

6. **Counts are theorem-status.** Load-bearing: seven mechanism classes; seven quadratic discriminants; (ℤ/2)³ = 8 elements (one identity, seven nontrivial); Fano plane PG(2, 𝔽₂) = 7 points; Steane [[7, 1, 3]]; |GL(3, 𝔽₂)| = 168 = 24 × 7. These all interconnect.

## Honest open status

- Bijection itself is complete and axiom-free.
- The CLAIM that the bijection is *structural* (not coincidental) depends on the manuscript-side argument about (ℤ/2)³ generators producing both the mechanism catalogue and the Fano plane. This argument is in `Fano_Plane_of_Arithmetic.md` and related Phase 2 papers. The kernel verifies the FORMAL bijection; the structural interpretation is mathematical-philosophical content.
- README's References section currently cites *Identity-Formation Bijection* and *Formation Universality v3* under PLACE-papers DOI; those papers are Phase 2 and not in the PLACE-papers v1.0.2 deposit. Citation correction pending.
